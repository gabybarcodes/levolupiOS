## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is my data private?**
Yes. All data stays on your device. Nothing is sent to external servers.

**How do I delete my account?**
Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes all your data from the device.

**I lost my data after reinstalling. Can I recover it?**
levolup stores data locally. If you didn't have an iCloud backup, the data cannot be recovered. We recommend keeping iCloud Backup enabled.

**The Share Extension isn't saving links. What do I do?**
Make sure you're sharing a URL (not just text). Tap the share icon in Safari → scroll to find levolup → tap it.
